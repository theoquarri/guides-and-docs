Welcome to Quarri. You should have an email inviting you to your Quarri account. Here's how to get going, in three steps.

1. Connect Quarri to Claude (about 5 minutes)
Sign in at app.quarri.ai with Google or Microsoft, or ask for a sign-in link by email. Then add Quarri to the Claude desktop app with the Quarri Onboarding Guide: https://theoquarri.github.io/guides-and-docs/Quarri_Onboarding_Guide.html
The first section is for everyone; the second is a one-off setup for your Claude admin.

2. Learn in the Quarri training space (about 10 minutes to start)
In Claude, type "Get started with Quarri". You get your own copy of a demo business, Finn Sawmill, with two years of data and public price data beside it. A quick tour first, then the Build track (a dashboard and its alert, an order desk app and a weekly Excel report) or the Connect track (how data comes in and gets organised). Nothing you do there touches your own data.

3. Build on your own data (when you're ready)
Back in your own workspace, ask for what you need in plain English, for example "What should we build first?" or "Email me when weekly sales drop more than 10%". The guided builder plans it with you, then builds it. Dashboards, apps and alerts are built in Claude Code, in the Claude desktop app.
The training space and guided builder: https://theoquarri.github.io/dashboards/Quarri-training-space-and-guided-builder.html
How to Use Quarri: https://theoquarri.github.io/guides-and-docs/How_to_Use_Quarri.html

If Claude reaches something it can't do, it can send your request straight to us. I'll also run a session with your team: just reply with a time that suits.
